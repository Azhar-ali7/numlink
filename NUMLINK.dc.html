How to play

Chain operations to transform the start number into the target. Every tap adds a link. Match par to earn the birdie.

1 Tap an operation (like ×3 or +7) to apply it to the current number.
2 The chain grows downward. The orange node is where you are now.
3 ÷ only works when it divides evenly — no fractions allowed.
4 Land exactly on the target to close the chain. Fewer moves = better.
